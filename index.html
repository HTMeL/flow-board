Flow Board

Flow Board is a simple Kanban board that runs in your browser. Organise work across four columns: Backlog, In Progress, In Review and Done.

Features

  • Drag and drop cards, or move them with buttons and the keyboard
  • Search by title or assignee and filter by priority
  • Overdue and due-soon highlighting
  • Undo for deleted cards
  • Export and import your board as JSON
  • No account needed: your cards are saved in your browser and never leave your device
